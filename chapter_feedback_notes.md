## Chapter 7: Deflation

### Engagement & Explanations
- **Brain Teaser**:
    - Scenario A: You owe $50k but can print $50k. (Groceries go up 15%).
    - Scenario B: You have $50k, groceries get 20% cheaper, but you lose 50% of income.
    - Scenario C: You have no debt, and things get cheaper. (The winner).
- **GDP Formula**: Explain GDP = Consumer Spending + Business Investment + Govt Spending + Net Exports.
    - Govt can inflate GDP just by spending more.
    - US is a consumer nation; they want you spending to keep GDP up.
- **Government Debt**: Govt has ~$40T debt. Inflation helps them service it; deflation would crush them.
- **Black Panthers**: Mention the Free Breakfast Program as an example of community organizing when the government fails.
- **Warning**: The government will not save you. They will step on you to accomplish their goals.
